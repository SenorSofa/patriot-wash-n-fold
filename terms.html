1. Introduction

Welcome to Patriot Wash N Fold! By using our services, you agree to these Terms of Service. Please read them carefully. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Services Provided

Patriot Wash N Fold offers laundry pick-up, washing, drying, folding, and delivery services to students, faculty, and staff at Dallas Baptist University (DBU). Our service includes the use of a Big Red laundry bag holding up to 22–25 lbs. Pricing is flat-rate regardless of bag weight, provided items fit within the bag.

3. Subscription Plans & Annual Billing

Annual Subscription Notice: The Red Plan and White Plan are annual subscriptions billed on a per-semester basis. Enrolling in either plan means you agree to be charged for both the fall and spring semesters of the current academic year, unless you cancel per Section 9. Subscriptions do not automatically renew to the following academic year — you must re-enroll each school year. The Blue Plan is a one-time purchase with no recurring charges.

Available Plans

  • Red Plan — Weekly Service: 16 washes per semester (one per active school week). $600 upfront per semester, or $40/week via recurring weekly payments ($640 total per semester).
  • White Plan — Bi-Weekly Service: 8 washes per semester (one every other active school week). $300 upfront per semester, or $40 every two weeks via recurring bi-weekly payments ($320 total per semester).
  • Blue Plan — Single Wash: One-time wash for $50. No subscription, no recurring charges.

Wash counts apply only to active school weeks when students are on campus. Service is not provided during official school holidays, winter break, spring break, or other periods when the university is not in regular session.

If you sign up during the fall semester, your Red or White Plan will apply to the spring semester. You will be charged the applicable semester rate at the start of spring. You may cancel before the spring charge by following Section 9.

4. Pricing & Tax

All prices displayed on our website are before applicable sales tax. Texas state sales tax (currently 8.25%) is calculated and added automatically at checkout via our payment processor, Stripe. The tax amount will be clearly shown before you complete your purchase.

5. Pick-Up and Delivery Schedule

Pick-up occurs every Tuesday from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, and delivery occurs on Thursdays from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM, both outside the Crowley dormitory at DBU. It is the customer's responsibility to ensure laundry is available for pick-up and retrieved during the delivery window.

If you miss the delivery window, you may designate someone else to pick up your bag, retrieve it from our Carrollton office, or it will be returned the following Tuesday. Times and locations are subject to change with at least 24 hours' notice.

6. Payment and Fees

Payment is required upon registration. We accept all major credit cards via Stripe. All fees are non-refundable except as provided in Sections 8 and 9. For recurring payment plans, your card on file is charged automatically on the billing schedule. Maintaining a valid payment method is your responsibility.

7. Use of Laundry Bag

Each customer receives a Big Red laundry bag holding up to 22–25 lbs. This bag must be used for all service requests. Items not in the provided bag will not be collected. A replacement bag costs $10.00 if lost or damaged.

8. Care and Handling of Laundry

We are not responsible for items that bleed, shrink, or change through normal washing; missing or damaged items unless caused by our negligence; delicate items without advance notice; non-washable clothing; or damage from foreign objects left in clothing. Specific care instructions must be communicated at sign-up or pick-up.

9. Service Errors

Notify us within 48 hours of delivery for any lost or damaged items. Our liability is limited to $50 per item and $200 per bag for loss or damage caused by our negligence.

10. Cancellations

Cancel with at least 24 hours' notice before your next scheduled pick-up. Refunds are issued for unused service beyond the current week. To cancel before a spring semester charge, notify us before the semester begins.

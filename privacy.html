Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up for or use Patriot Wash N Fold services, we may collect the following information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Dorm and room number at Dallas Baptist University
  • Parent or guardian email address
  • Payment information (processed securely through Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • Service preferences (detergent type, special care instructions)
  • Communications you send us via email, phone, or our contact form

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and manage your laundry pick-up and delivery service
  • Process payments and send billing confirmations
  • Send service reminders and notifications via text and email
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Improve our services and operations

3. Payment Processing

All payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. Patriot Wash N Fold does not store, transmit, or have access to your full credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment information. Sales tax is calculated and collected automatically at checkout in accordance with Texas state law.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Stripe, for payment processing
  • Our CRM platform (GoHighLevel), for managing your account and communications
  • Service providers who assist in operating our business, under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies if required by law

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text message reminders and notifications related to your laundry service (pickup reminders, delivery confirmations, and account updates). Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by texting STOP to our number or contacting us directly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@patriotwashnfold.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
